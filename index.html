About

Automation for food & beverage, from code to start-up

Industrial Automation Engineer with 5 years of experience delivering PLC, HMI/SCADA and process control solutions for large-scale food and beverage projects in Vietnam and abroad, for global brands including Nestlé, Suntory PepsiCo and Dong-A Otsuka.

Proficient in Siemens S7-1500 / WinCC and Allen-Bradley ControlLogix / AVEVA, covering the full project lifecycle: requirements, software development, Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT) with customers, site commissioning, production start-up and handover.

How I deliver

One engineer across the whole project lifecycle

I follow each system from the first specification to the moment operators run it in production.

  1. Requirements

    Clarify process needs and control specifications with the customer and process team.

  2. Develop

    PLC logic in Ladder and SCL / Structured Text, plus SCADA / HMI screens.

  3. FAT

    Factory Acceptance Test with the customer, run in English.

    Customer sign-off

  4. Commissioning

    I/O checkout, water testing, passivation and CIP testing on site.

  5. Start-up

    Support production start-up and troubleshoot until the line runs stably.

  6. Handover

    Documentation, system handover and operator training.
